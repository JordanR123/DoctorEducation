# Online Professional Doctorates for Canadians — Research Report

A comparison of **online, part-time, fully asynchronous doctoral programs** (DBA, EdD, DMin, DProf, etc.) that a **Canadian student** can complete **entirely remotely**, where the culminating work is **an applied project / portfolio / thesis-by-publication rather than a traditional dissertation**, and the institution is **non-profit (not for-profit)**.

> **Research date:** June 2026. Every program below was checked against official university pages (cited). The three filters that eliminate most programs are: **(a) fully asynchronous** (no required live class sessions), **(b) no residency / no campus visits**, and **(c) an applied capstone, not a 5-chapter dissertation**.

---

## Screening criteria (all six required)

1. **Open to Canadians** — the school need not be in Canada, but must admit Canadian/international students.
2. **Online + part-time.**
3. **No in-person meetings / no residency / no on-campus intensives** — 100% remote.
4. **Fully asynchronous** — no required scheduled live/synchronous class sessions.
5. **No traditional dissertation** — an applied doctoral project, dissertation-in-practice, ministry project, portfolio, or thesis-by-publication instead.
6. **Not for-profit** — public or private non-profit only (University of Phoenix, Capella, Walden, Northcentral, Aspen, etc. excluded; Liberty University is non-profit and eligible).

### Two important caveats that apply across the whole table

- **Oral defense / viva ≠ a class session.** Almost every program here (US applied projects *and* UK doctorates) ends with **one synchronous oral defense or viva**. That is a single scheduled oral event, not synchronous coursework, so these still meet criterion 4 — but if you want *zero* live events of any kind, there is essentially **no reputable non-profit doctorate that qualifies**. Each program's defense requirement is noted.
- **Currency:** tuition is collected in the original currency and converted to CAD at **approximate June 2026 rates (USD→CAD ≈ 1.37, GBP→CAD ≈ 1.74)**. Treat CAD figures as *estimates*; exchange rates and tuition both move. Fees (technology, resource, graduation) are usually extra.

6. **Carey** — "fully online, no residency, incl. graduation"; self-paced 10-wk courses (async implied, not verbatim). Capstone labeled "thesis" — confirm it counts as applied/ministry. **Best Canada-based & lowest-cost overall.**
7. **Middlesex** — strongest work-based, non-dissertation fit; "no residencies, summer schools or block teaching," only an online induction day. Tuition not published — quote via `professionals@mdx.ac.uk`.

## Reference table — capstone / culminating-project types vs. a traditional dissertation

The user's rule 5 excludes a *traditional dissertation*. Many programs above use a renamed or restructured capstone. Here is what each type actually is, and how it compares to a classic PhD dissertation on **rigor, length, originality requirement, structure, and defense**.

| Capstone type | What it is | Length / structure | Originality requirement | Defense | **vs. traditional dissertation** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Traditional 5-chapter dissertation** *(baseline)* | Original empirical research filling a **gap in scholarly literature** to create new theory. The PhD standard. | 5 chapters (Intro, Lit Review, Methods, Findings, Discussion); ~150–300+ pp / UK 60–80k words. | Must make an **original contribution to theoretical knowledge.** | Yes — formal 2–3 hr defense/viva. | **The benchmark.** Most theory-oriented; still perceived as the "gold standard" for research-academic careers. |
| **Dissertation-in-Practice (DiP)** — CPED / EdD model | Scholarly work addressing a complex **Problem of Practice** embedded in the candidate's profession. | Often keeps the 5-chapter shape but **narrower & more applied**; may be improvement-science report, evaluation, or portfolio. | Advances knowledge **in the field of practice**; judged by impact on practice, not a literature gap. | Yes — proposal + final oral defense. | **Comparable rigor, more applied.** "Still rigorous…more structured & defined in scope." Positioned as *equal but practice-oriented*, not lesser. |
| **Applied Doctoral Project / Doctoral Study** | Identify a real problem → methodology → project → findings → **a practical, implementable solution.** | Structured, problem-driven (proposal → data → solution). | Contribution is a **practical/applied solution** demonstrating research competence. | Yes — proposal + final oral defense (~20-min presentation + Q&A). | **Same demonstrated competence, applied vs. theoretical.** Similar rigor, focused on application. |
| **Problem-of-Practice / Action-Research project** | Investigate & **solve a problem in your own workplace/practice** (action research, improvement science, evaluation). | Built around **iterative PDSA-style cycles** documenting intervention & results. | Original **contextual** contribution (improved outcomes in a specific setting), not generalizable theory. | Yes — proposal + oral defense. | **Rigorous but practice-embedded.** Valued for organizational impact over theoretical generalizability. |
| **Ministry Project / Thesis (DMin)** | A major designed-and-implemented **ministry project** in the candidate's area of practice ("Project Dissertation"). | Written report of the project; governed by **ATS** standards + seminary methodology. | A **credible formal research element** contributing to ministry practice. | Yes — ~1 hr final oral defense (advisor + faculty + outside examiner). | **Applied ministry capstone**, professionally rigorous within ATS; applied rather than theoretical. |
| **Portfolio of practice / Portfolio thesis** | Curated **collection of professional/public works** + a critical commentary theorizing their significance. | Portfolio + **25,000–45,000-word critical commentary**. | Doctoral-level **original contribution through the body of practice itself.** | Yes — viva before a chaired panel (usually 2 external + 1 internal). | Treated as the **"professional equivalent to the PhD" with the same assessment criteria** (e.g. Middlesex). Equal rigor, evidenced through accumulated practice. |
| **Thesis by Publication / PhD by Published Work** | Doctoral recognition of a **pre-existing body of peer-reviewed publications**, bound by a synthesizing commentary. | Published outputs + integrating commentary (~10–20k words); some routes ~2 yrs. | Publication itself is treated as **peer-validated evidence of original, significant research.** | Yes — viva on originality/rigour/contribution. | **Equivalent in standard to a traditional PhD;** same contribution (new knowledge) but evidenced retrospectively via publications. Requires an existing publication record. |
| **Doctoral consulting / business project (DBA)** | Applied, **action-research / consulting-style** investigation of a real organizational problem → actionable management knowledge. | DBA thesis (UK ~50–60k words) or portfolio of applied projects; often a qualifying-report milestone. | Original **contribution to professional practice** (and often applied theory). | Yes — viva(s); e.g. Aston: qualifying-report viva + final thesis viva. | Per **AACSB**, the DBA is **not less rigorous — differently oriented** (PhD = theoretical; DBA = applied/practice). Market *perception* still favors the PhD for research institutions. |

**Key cross-cutting insight:** modern applied doctorates (DiP, doctoral study, DBA project, DMin project, portfolio, thesis-by-publication) are generally framed by their grad schools and by bodies like **CPED** and **AACSB** as **equally rigorous to a PhD dissertation but oriented toward professional practice rather than theory-building**. The main practical differences are **orientation** (solve a real problem vs. fill a literature gap) and sometimes **scope/length**, not a lower standard. The lingering perception that the dissertation is "superior" reflects its fit for *academic-research careers*, not greater quality.

## Bottom line

**Cleanest full passes** (explicitly asynchronous + explicitly no residency, from official pages):
- **Liberty University** — DBA, EdD in Christian Leadership, DMin *(Christian; DMin is the cheapest at ≈ CA$16,900)*
- **Touro University Worldwide DBA** *(not Christian; clearest async wording, ≈ CA$46,000, ~2 yrs)*
- **Grace Theological DMin** *(Christian; ≈ CA$27,000)*
- **Knox Theological DMin** *(Christian; ≈ CA$29,600)*

**Best Canada-based / lowest cost overall:** **Carey Theological College DMin** — CA$13,500, fully online, no residency *(confirm no live sessions and that the "thesis" counts as an applied/ministry project)*.

**Best non-dissertation, work-based fit (international):** **Middlesex Professional Practice DProf (Business)** — most flexible work-based capstone, online induction only *(request the tuition figure)*; **Aston Executive DBA** — fully async with a published £39,000 fee, but the output is a thesis with two online vivas.

**Qualified passes to verify by email before applying:** Trevecca (Zoom optional?), Regent DMin (asynchronous?), MBTS DMin (residency/capstone/tuition?), Carey (live sessions / thesis type?).

**One prudent last step for any program:** email the program advisor and ask in one line — *"Are there zero required synchronous sessions and zero required campus visits for the entire degree?"* Third-party ranking sites repeatedly mislabel these details; only the official answer counts.
